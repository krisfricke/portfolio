Just four days after the Advanced Varroa Workshop that

followed the VAA conference in frosty Beechworth, the

about twenty or so of us who are compelled to attend

every state beekeeping conference found ourselves

boarding flights once again, this time anticipating much

warmer weather in Gold Coast, QLD.

The 121% annual Queensland Beekeepers Association began with

formal AGM business. There are currently 10,000 beekeepers in QLD

running 160,000 hives. Jacob Stevens has been QBA president for a

number of years now, which he mentioned has been “at great cost to

my family and business” and that he would decline to be nominated

again.

In light of this obvious burden a motion was later made and

passed to allow the QBA board to hire a paid “president’s assistant.”
