As with the other conferences there were a variety of presentations

on varroa, pollination and small hive beetle. A particularly worthwhile

occurrence was a panel of case studies from varroa positive NSW.

Jon Lockwood, Ben Laybutt and Daryl Brenton each presented about

their operations and how they were affected by varroa, and then took

questions from the floor. One reoccurring theme that came up here and

had already been alluded to a few times during the conference is the fact

that beekeepers more or less have to use off label slow release oxalic

strips to stay on top of the mites. Many have expressed that only using

the registered miticides, and following the label constraints is impossible

or at best would result in back to back Bayvarol use which in actual fact

is worse for the industry than the off label treatments.
