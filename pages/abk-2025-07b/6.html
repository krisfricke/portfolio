The next day, Saturday (by which

point some of us had been

Beechworth five days, feeling like We were growing roots) was one of the

Advanced Varroa Management Workshops, of which there seems to be

one attached to every state conference. This differed substantially from the

original varroa workshops, with a section on financial planning for varroa

and several well produced videos about case studies of beekeepers who

have already been dealing with varroa.

After this the last of us could finally disperse. No visit to Beechworth

is complete without a visit to the very nice Beechworth Honey shop

where not surprisingly we ran into a number of other beekeepers doing

the same thing.

Beechworth was and isa lovely little town, bundling up with coats and

scarves and drinking mulled wine at the Hotel Nicholas is fun for a week

but I'm looking forward to the Queensland conference, which

I leave for

only two days after arriving home from this (though it'll probably not make

this magazine issue), for a week in the sun!
